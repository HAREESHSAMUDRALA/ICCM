# ICCM 2023 — GNN for Super-Resolution Alzheimer's Detection

📄 Paper presented at the **9th International Conference on Computers, Management & Mathematical Sciences (ICCM 2023)**, organized by the North Eastern Regional Institute of Science and Technology (NERIST), Arunachal Pradesh, India, in collaboration with emlyon Business School (France), California State University (USA), and IAASSE.

## Overview

**Title:** A Graphical Neural Network (GNN) Model for Super Resolution Images to Detect Alzheimer's Disease
**Author:** Samudrala Hareesh — Electronics and Communication Engineering, National Institute of Technology Andhra Pradesh, Tadepalligudem
**Session:** Technical Session 6 — Mathematical Computation & Management Applications (ICCM 2023-123)
**Mode:** Online, 24–25 August 2023

## About

This repository contains the paper presented at ICCM 2023, proposing a Graph Neural Network (GNN)-based approach to enhance image resolution for improved early detection of Alzheimer's disease from medical imaging data.
